================================
    DATA
================================

The data is from: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720

The values of the 16-bit unsigned heightmap correspond to half-meters
So a value of 100 would mean 50 meters
The unsigned heightmap is also offset by +20000 to make the values positive
That means the real elevation relative to sea level is (x*0.5) - 20000
Sea level is 1727400 meters

The illumination maps are just textures to colour the surface like is visible to humans
They aren't reflective of the actual values recorded
There is a 1k, 2k, and 4k version, each are identical just at different resolutions
